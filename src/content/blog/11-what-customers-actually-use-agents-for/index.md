---
title: "What customers actually use our agents for"
description: "Three real deployments: replacing a call centre, answering order questions, and one that was set up elaborately and then stopped."
---

It's easy to write about AI agents in general. Here is what a few customers did with ours, without names.

## They replaced their call centre

One customer used our autonomous agent to run support over WhatsApp, and shut down their phone call centre. They moved their whole support operation to WhatsApp.

What I notice is that it wasn't a pilot alongside the old system. They switched. For that to work, the agent had to hand off to people for the cases it couldn't handle, which is why handoff matters so much to us.

## They let customers ask about products and orders

A large manufacturer uses our agents so their own customers can ask about products and look up their orders. It's the unglamorous use case, and it's probably the most common one: answer the question that would otherwise go to a support person.

## One that was set up elaborately and then stopped

Another customer built their whole business workflow across several agents. They aren't using it now.

I'd rather include this than only tell the good stories. A setup with many agents is more impressive to look at than a single agent that answers a few questions well, and I suspect the simple setups last longer. I don't have proof of that. It's what I've seen.

## What I take from these

- The most successful uses replaced something concrete: a call centre, a stack of repetitive questions.
- Handoff to people has to work from day one, or nobody switches off the old system.
- A deployment that looks complex isn't evidence that it's working.
