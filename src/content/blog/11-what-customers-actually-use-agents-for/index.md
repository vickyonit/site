Here is what a few customers did with our agents.

## They replaced their call centre

One customer uses our autonomous agent for support and shut down their entire call centre. They moved to a support system based on WhatsApp.

## They let customers ask about products and orders

A large manufacturer uses our agents so their customers can ask about products and the orders they've placed.

## One that was set up elaborately and then stopped

Another customer set up their entire business workflow using multiple agents. They aren't using it anymore.
