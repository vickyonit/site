---
title: "Why our agents run on Gemini"
description: "How we picked a model for our WhatsApp agents."
---

Our agents run on Gemini. When we started building them in 2025, it was the cheaper model compared to the others. We also tried OpenAI's models, and on cost we were getting similar performance from Gemini itself, so we stayed.

## Why cost mattered

WhatsApp conversations are frequent and short. Across thousands of conversations a month, a small difference in cost per message adds up to a real difference in what a customer can afford to run.

## The caveat

"Similar performance" was judged on our own conversations and our own tasks. It's not a general claim about the models.
