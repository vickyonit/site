---

A lot of small businesses run customer service from a single WhatsApp Business app number. The phone, or a few phones, sit with different staff. It works until you want to know anything.

## What the owner can't see

Nobody knows what customers are asking. Nobody knows how staff replied, or how fast, or whether someone promised something they shouldn't have. That includes the owner. The information exists, but it's spread across phones and chat histories that nobody can search.

## What we built

We call it CoPilot. A business connects its existing WhatsApp Business app number. It syncs the conversations. Then, instead of giving the owner another dashboard to learn, they connect their own AI assistant to it, whether that's Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, through MCP.

Setup is three steps: connect the number, sync, connect the assistant.

After that the owner can just ask. What are customers asking about this week? Which questions are we slow to answer? How did my staff handle that complaint?

## Why I like this design

We didn't build a new interface. Owners already use an assistant for other things, so we gave it access to the one part of their business they couldn't see.

It also means the product isn't tied to one model. If a customer prefers a different assistant next year, the connection works the same way.

## What it doesn't do

It reads and explains. It doesn't make the business's replies better or faster by itself. That's a different product, and it's the one our autonomous agents are for.
