A lot of small businesses run customer service from a single WhatsApp Business app number, shared across several people. Nobody knows what's happening on it, including the owner. What are customers asking? How are staff replying? It's all spread across phones and chat histories that nobody can search.

## What we built

We call it CoPilot. A business onboards its existing WhatsApp Business app number, and we sync its conversations. Then the owner connects their own AI assistant, whether that's Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, to Peach CoPilot through MCP.

Setup is three steps: connect the number, sync, and connect the assistant.

After that, the owner can ask their assistant what customers are asking about, and how their people are replying.

## Why through MCP

Owners already use these assistants. Instead of building another dashboard to learn, we gave their assistant access to the one part of the business they couldn't see.
